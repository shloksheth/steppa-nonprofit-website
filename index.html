What does “Ship It” mean?

Finish a small project, test it, explain how it works, and share a demo in the planned showcase. Publishing code, a name, or a photo will need separate permission. A finished project matters more than a perfect one.

02 / The pilot at a glance

Your first step,
with a plan.

A small first cohort gives us room to learn what actually helps beginners finish.

See what happens before enrollment ↓
In preparation · enrollment is not open
Who it’s for
Beginners ages 13–18, especially learners with limited access to structured coding support. No previous programming experience needed.
Cost
The planned course and learning support are free.
Where
Online. Orlando-area meetups are a later possibility, once a venue and supervision arrangements are confirmed.
How it works
Four project-based modules, one moderated online group session each week, and structured help between sessions.
What you’ll need
Access to a laptop or desktop and an internet connection for coding. Early exercises are intended to run in a browser. A phone is useful for reading this site, but won’t replace a computer for projects.
Dates & time
The start date, session times, course length, and expected weekly workload are still being finalized. These will be published before applications open.

03 / People behind the progress

Getting stuck
is part of learning.

The pilot is being designed around human support. Mentors will help learners understand a problem and work toward their own answer.

What help will look like

  1. Bring the project.

    Use the weekly group session to talk through a bug, ask a question, or show what you tried.

  2. Get a next step.

    Mentors give explanations and progressive hints so the learner remains the person doing the building.

  3. Keep going between sessions.

    The plan includes a moderated help channel. Its support hours and response expectations will be published before the pilot begins.

For parents & guardians

Clear boundaries.
Before anyone joins.

These are the proposed safeguards being developed for the pilot:

  • Group support in approved, moderated spaces; no unobserved adult–minor meetings or private messaging by default.
  • Role-based volunteer screening and training, with a named safety contact and reporting process.
  • Recording off by default; separate permissions for sharing projects, names, images, or stories.
  • A privacy notice explaining what information is needed, who can access it, and when it is deleted.

The full participation policies and contact details are not published yet. They must be available for review before learner applications or sessions begin.

A few more things

Before your
first chapter.

Can I join if I’ve never written code?

Yes—the planned Python pilot starts with the basics. You do not need a portfolio or previous course. You do need access to a computer, time to practice, and a willingness to ask questions.

Will Steppa provide a computer?

Device access is part of the longer-term mission, but device distribution is not available in the first pilot as currently planned. Eligibility, inventory, repair, and data-wiping procedures need to be in place first.

Can learners under 13 take part?

The first pilot is being planned for ages 13–18. Younger learners are outside its current scope; age-appropriate consent and communication systems would need to be ready before that changes.

Do I have to publish my name or photo?

The pilot plan calls for separate permission for code, names, images, voice, and testimonials, with an option to publish under a chosen name. Those choices will be explained in the participation policies before enrollment.

Are web or game development courses open?

No. The first focus is one complete Python course. Further tracks will depend on learner demand, volunteer capacity, and what the pilot teaches us.

How can I apply or contact the team?

Applications and a public program contact route are not available yet. This page will be updated with contact details, the schedule, and participation policies before enrollment opens. Donations are available separately through HCB below.
